SoD Uploader

SoD Uploader is an internal tool made by UpworldFX. It publishes episodes and short videos of The School of Drugs, an educational animated series inspired by the book Drugs: A User's Guide by Mike Haskins (Ebury Press, 2003), to the series' own YouTube channel, and reads that channel's statistics into a private production dashboard.

It is used only by the channel's owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to anyone else.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
