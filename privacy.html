Privacy policy

This policy covers SoD Uploader, an internal tool operated by UpworldFX ("we").

YouTube API Services

SoD Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By authorising it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

The tool has one user: the owner of The School of Drugs YouTube channel. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and edit them afterwards, on the authorised channel only.
  • Read-only access to that channel's YouTube Analytics (for example views, watch time and subscribers).

How the data is used and stored

Uploads go to the authorised channel only. Analytics figures are shown in a private dashboard seen only by the channel owner. The sign-in token Google issues is stored on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

SoD Uploader does not place cookies or tracking technology on anyone's device.

Retention and deletion

Stored analytics figures are kept only while they are needed for the dashboard and are deleted on request, or within 30 days of access being revoked. You can revoke SoD Uploader's access at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
